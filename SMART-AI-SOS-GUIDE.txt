LIFELINE AI - SMART ONE-TAP SOS UPGRADE
========================================

WHAT WAS ADDED
1. A large Smart AI SOS button on the patient portal.
2. Five-second cancellation countdown to prevent accidental alerts.
3. High-accuracy GPS detection.
4. Automatic reverse geocoding with coordinate fallback.
5. Nearest-hospital selection using geographic distance.
6. Automatic unknown-patient/limited-details emergency creation.
7. Critical severity, score 100, AI triage metadata and immediate-response flag.
8. Existing hospital acceptance, driver assignment, dispatch, arrival and completion flow is reused.
9. Existing live status tracker updates automatically through Firestore.
10. SOS alerts are visually highlighted in the hospital panel.

TEST
- Open login.html with Live Server.
- Login as PATIENT or ADMIN.
- Open index.html.
- Press Smart AI SOS, allow location, and wait or press Send Now.
- Login to the selected hospital in another browser/profile.
- The critical SOS appears automatically.
- Accept it, assign a driver, and complete the same normal workflow.

IMPORTANT
- Browsers allow accurate location only on localhost or HTTPS.
- The user must grant location permission.
- No existing emergency, hospital, driver, billing, authentication or analytics code was removed.
